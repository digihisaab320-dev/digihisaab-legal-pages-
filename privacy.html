Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This policy explains how DigiHisaab handles information when you use the app.

Information you enter

Depending on the features available, you may enter business information such as product names, categories, stock, sales, prices, customer names, phone numbers and khata/payment records. Enter only information you are authorized to manage.

Storage and use

DigiHisaab is intended to help you manage shop records on your device. The exact storage and backup behavior depends on the app version and enabled features. We do not claim that records are uploaded to a DigiHisaab-operated server unless the app clearly says so.

Google Drive backup

If an optional Google Drive backup feature is available and you choose to connect an account, backup files may be created or restored in the Google Drive account you authorize. Those files are in your account and subject to Google's terms and privacy practices. You can manage the files in your Drive and revoke app access in your Google Account settings. Do not assume a backup completed unless the app confirms success.

Sharing

You may choose to share or export information using device or app features. The recipients you choose will handle shared information under their own practices. DigiHisaab does not sell your shop records.

Security and retention

Protect your device and Google account. No electronic storage method is completely secure. Records may be deleted using available in-app controls, by clearing app data or uninstalling the app; this may remove records that have not been backed up. Manage Drive backup files directly in your own Drive.

Children

DigiHisaab is a business tool and is not intended for children.

Policy changes

We may update this policy as app features change. The updated date will appear on this page.
